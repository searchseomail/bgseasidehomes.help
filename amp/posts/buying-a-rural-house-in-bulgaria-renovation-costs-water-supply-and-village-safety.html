ZenithGamer OP

My partner and I are seriously considering buying an abandoned or partially renovated village house in rural Bulgaria for retirement. We are trying to figure out realistic renovation costs per square meter, especially for older brick properties, and whether drilling a private well is usually necessary for reliable water supply. Additionally, how safe are most villages in regions like Veliko Tarnovo or Vratsa regarding break-ins when the property is left empty for months at a time?
