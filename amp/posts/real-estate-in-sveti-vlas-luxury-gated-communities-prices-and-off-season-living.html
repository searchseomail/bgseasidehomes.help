Maintenance fees are the big kicker in Sveti Vlas luxury complexes. They usually run anywhere from 10 to 18 EUR per square meter per year. In some of the ultra-high-end gated communities with multiple pools, spas, and 24/7 security, it can even push past 20 EUR/sqm annually. Make sure you factor that into your yearly budget!

To answer your question about the off-season: yes, Sveti Vlas is very quiet from November to April, but it is definitely not completely deserted like some parts of Sunny Beach. There is a core community of year-round residents—mostly locals, some expats, and remote workers who enjoy the peaceful sea air. However, expect most restaurants and beach bars to be closed.
