First-Line Sea View Apartments in Bulgaria: Is the Extra Cost Really Worth It?

StaticPulse OP
09.02.2025 04:01
I'm currently looking into buying a holiday property on the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, particularly around Sunny Beach or Sveti Vlas. Agents are heavily pushing first-line sea view apartments, but the price markup compared to second or third-line properties is often 30% to 50% higher. For those of you who own or rent these frontline places, do you find the unobstructed views and proximity to the beach justify the massive price difference, or do the issues with high humidity and summer noise outweigh the benefits?

We bought a first-line apartment in Sveti Vlas five years ago, and honestly, the views are incredible every single morning. However, the agents downplay the humidity. Our metal balcony railings and AC units rusted much faster than expected. If you have the budget, the view is worth it, but just be prepared for extra maintenance.
