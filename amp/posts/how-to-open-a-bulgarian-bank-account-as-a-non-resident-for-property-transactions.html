As a non-EU citizen, opening an account can be tricky depending on the bank. Postbank and UniCredit Bulbank are generally considered the most foreigner-friendly for non-residents purchasing real estate. You will definitely need your valid passport, a reference letter from your home bank, and preliminary purchase contract for the Sofia apartment.

You can theoretically use a Power of Attorney (POA) issued to a local lawyer, but many compliance departments at banks like Fibank or DSK will reject remote applications for non-EU nationals unless you have a registered residence status or a very compelling reason. Doing it in person is much faster.
