Studio vs One-Bedroom Apartment: Which Has Better Resale Liquidity in Bulgaria?

VelocityGhost
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 04:16
I am currently looking to invest in Bulgarian real estate, specifically focusing on Sofia and the coastal areas, with an exit strategy in mind for 5-7 years down the line. I'm torn between buying a cheaper studio apartment or stretching my budget slightly for a standard one-bedroom unit. Which of these two property types typically offers better resale liquidity and faster time on the market in the current economic climate? I would love to hear experiences from local agents or seasoned investors who have recently sold properties here.

Replied: 19.01.2025 12:27
As a local agent in Sofia, I can tell you that studios and one-bedroom apartments both move relatively fast, but one-bedrooms generally attract a much wider pool of serious buyers looking for long-term homes, whereas studios are mostly bought by investors or students.

If you are looking at the coastal areas, keep in mind that maintenance fees can eat into your profits, and you should definitely read up on living on the Bulgarian coast in winter before deciding on the size, as heating a poorly insulated studio or one-bed is a completely different ballgame.
