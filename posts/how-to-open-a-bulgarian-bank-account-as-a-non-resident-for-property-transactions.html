How to Open a Bulgarian Bank Account as a Non-Resident for Property Transactions

ApexPredator
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.01.2025 13:11
I am a non-EU citizen currently in the process of buying an apartment in Sofia and I need to open a local bank account to transfer the funds and pay property taxes. Which banks are currently the most foreigner-friendly for non-residents, and what is the exact list of documents required? Do I need to be physically present in Bulgaria, or can this process be completed remotely via a Power of Attorney?

Replies (8)

IronPhoenix
Replied: 27.04.2025 13:43
As a non-EU citizen, opening an account can be tricky depending on the bank. Postbank and UniCredit Bulbank are generally considered the most foreigner-friendly for non-residents purchasing real estate. You will definitely need your valid passport, a reference letter from your home bank, and preliminary purchase contract for the Sofia apartment.
ZenithDrifter
Replied: 08.05.2025 13:23
When I bought my place last year, I had to be physically present in Sofia. Most Bulgarian banks have strict KYC policies and strongly prefer in-person appointments for non-residents, even if you try to use a Power of Attorney. It took about two visits to get everything finalized.

You can theoretically use a Power of Attorney (POA) issued to a local lawyer, but many compliance departments at banks like Fibank or DSK will reject remote applications for non-EU nationals unless you have a registered residence status or a very compelling reason. Doing it in person is much faster.
VortexSurfer
Replied: 15.10.2025 16:06
DSK Bank and UniCredit are usually the go-to choices for property buyers. Along with your passport, they asked me for proof of the origin of funds (like a recent tax return or sale of another asset) because of strict anti-money laundering regulations.
