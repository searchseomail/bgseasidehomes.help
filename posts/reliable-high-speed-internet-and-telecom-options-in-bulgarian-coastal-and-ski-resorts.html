Reliable High-Speed Internet and Telecom Options in Bulgarian Coastal and Ski Resorts

EchoMatrix
Topic Starter
Posted: 27.01.2025 02:32
I am planning to spend a few months working remotely from both Bansko and Sunny Beach this year, and I need a stable, high-speed internet connection for daily video calls. Are the local fiber optic networks reliable in these resort areas, or should I rely on 5G mobile data plans as a backup? Which telecom providers—Vivacom, A1, or Yettel—offer the best coverage and foreigner-friendly contract terms for temporary residents?

I worked remotely from Bansko last winter and the local fiber optic networks are actually surprisingly fast and reliable. Most apartments marketed for digital nomads have dedicated fiber lines. I never had an issue with daily Zoom calls.

Vivacom has been my go-to provider for years. Their fiber coverage is extensive in Bansko, and their customer service agents in the Sofia and regional offices usually speak decent English.
