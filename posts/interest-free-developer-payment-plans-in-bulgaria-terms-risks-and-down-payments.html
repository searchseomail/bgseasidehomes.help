Interest-Free Developer Payment Plans in Bulgaria: Terms, Risks, and Down Payments

NeonSpectre
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.01.2025 01:14
I am currently looking into buying an off-plan apartment in Sofia and noticed several developers offering 0% interest deferred payment plans tied to construction milestones. While this sounds much better than taking a traditional mortgage right away, I'm concerned about hidden clauses, developer reliability, and what happens if there are construction delays. What are the typical down payment percentages required, and what major risks should I watch out for in the contract terms before signing?

Typically, developers in Sofia ask for 10% to 20% down upon signing the preliminary contract, with the rest split across milestones like Act 14, Act 15, and Act 16.
