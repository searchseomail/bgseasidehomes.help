Borovets vs Pamporovo: Which Bulgarian Ski Resort Offers Better Real Estate ROI?

RetroWave
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.02.2025 08:03
I am currently looking into the Bulgarian ski property market and trying to decide whether Borovets or Pamporovo presents a better investment opportunity for long-term ROI and rental yields. Borovets has proximity to Sofia and established prestige, while Pamporovo is often praised for its family-friendly vibe and lower entry prices. I'd love to hear from investors or locals who have experience with property management, occupancy rates, and capital appreciation in both resorts. Which one currently offers the smartest balance of cost and return?
