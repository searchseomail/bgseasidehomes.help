Maintenance Fees in Bulgarian Seaside Resorts: Typical Costs and How to Avoid Overpaying

ApexPredator
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.01.2025 16:15
I am currently looking at buying an apartment in Sunny Beach, but I keep hearing horror stories about exorbitant yearly maintenance fees charged by complex management. What are the typical per-square-meter costs I should realistically expect for a standard one-bedroom flat in complexes with a pool and security? More importantly, are these fees negotiable, and what legal recourse do owners have if the management company increases them arbitrarily every year?

Sunny Beach is notorious for high maintenance fees. For a standard one-bedroom with a pool and security, you are realistically looking at anywhere from 8 to 12 euros per square meter per year. Some luxury complexes even push past 15 euros!

Legally, management companies are supposed to vote on fee increases at the general meeting of owners (AGM). However, many developers retain a controlling number of votes through unsold apartments, so they just push whatever increases they want.

Can you sue them for arbitrary increases? Technically yes, under the Bulgarian Condominium Ownership Management Act, you can challenge decisions at court if they violate the law, but litigation takes years and costs a fortune.
CyberNomad

Some owners band together, legally form an Etapazhna Sobstvenost (homeowners association), and fire the management company to self-manage or hire a cheaper firm. It's messy, but it's the only real recourse.
