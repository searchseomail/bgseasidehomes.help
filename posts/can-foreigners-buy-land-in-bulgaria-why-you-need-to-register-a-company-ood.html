Can Foreigners Buy Land in Bulgaria? Why You Need to Register a Company (OOD)

EchoKnight
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.02.2025 03:34
I am a non-EU citizen looking to purchase a rural property with a large plot of land in Bulgaria. I've heard that while foreigners can buy buildings freely, owning agricultural or forest land directly in an individual's name is legally restricted. Could someone explain the exact rules and whether registering an OOD (Limited Liability Company) is the best workaround to bypass this restriction?
