How to Evaluate an HOA and Complex Management Company Before Purchasing a Resort Flat

LunarTides
Topic Starter
Posted: 26.01.2025 16:02
I am currently looking into buying a resort flat, but I keep hearing horror stories about mismanaged HOAs and terrible property management companies ruining the investment. What specific financial documents, reserve studies, and operational metrics should I request and review before making an offer? How can I effectively gauge the management company's responsiveness and maintenance track record prior to signing the contract?

Replies (10)

This is a massive issue with resort flats, especially in places where maintenance fees can suddenly skyrocket. Always ask for the last three years of audited financial statements and the current reserve study to see if they are actually funding long-term repairs.
TitanForge
Replied: 20.05.2025 11:32
Before you even make an offer, demand to see the minutes from the last 12 months of HOA meetings. That is where all the secret drama, upcoming special assessments, and neighbor complaints are actually documented.

To test the management company's responsiveness, try walking around the complex unannounced and ask a random owner about how fast they fix things. You can also send a test email to their support desk with a hypothetical maintenance question and time how long it takes them to reply.

Make sure you check the percentage of delinquent owners in the complex. If more than 15 or 20 percent of the owners are behind on their HOA dues, the management company is going to start cutting corners on amenities and maintenance very quickly.
