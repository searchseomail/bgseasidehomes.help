Home Insurance in Bulgaria: Average Annual Premiums and Earthquake Coverage

CosmicDrifter
Topic Starter
Posted: 20.01.2025 16:26
I am currently looking to buy property insurance for my apartment in Sofia and trying to figure out what realistic costs to expect. What is the average annual premium I should budget for standard coverage, and how does earthquake insurance usually factor into the overall policy? I want to make sure I am adequately protected given the seismic history here without overpaying for unnecessary add-ons. Any insights from local homeowners or insurance brokers would be greatly appreciated.

Replies (11)

BlazeWalker
Replied: 17.02.2025 13:34
For a standard apartment in Sofia, you can generally expect to pay anywhere between 100 to 250 BGN per year for basic coverage. It really depends on the square footage and the insured value of the property.
LunarTides
Replied: 23.02.2025 10:27
Earthquake insurance is usually an optional add-on with some insurers, while others include it in their comprehensive packages. Given Sofia's seismic history, definitely make sure it's explicitly included in your policy.
PhantomPixel
Replied: 03.03.2025 14:11
When I was setting up my apartment insurance, adding earthquake risk only increased the annual premium by about 30 to 50 BGN, which is totally worth the peace of mind.
SolarKnight
Replied: 09.05.2025 09:27
Make sure to check the deductibles carefully. Some cheaper policies have a massive deductible for earthquake damage, meaning you'd pay a huge chunk out of pocket before the insurer pitches in.

Most Bulgarian banks will require you to have property insurance if you have a mortgage, and they almost always insist on earthquake coverage as part of the collateral terms.

I pay around 180 BGN annually for a 3-room apartment in Lozenets. That covers fire, water damage from neighbors, and a solid earthquake rider.
