Can Foreign Non-Residents Get a Mortgage in Bulgarian Banks? Interest Rates and Requirements

ByteRunner
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.01.2025 04:04
I am a non-EU citizen looking to purchase a holiday apartment in Bansko, but I currently live and work abroad. Is it possible for a foreign non-resident to secure financing through a Bulgarian bank, and what documentation will I need to provide? Additionally, I would like to know what kind of interest rates and LTV (loan-to-value) ratios I should realistically expect for this type of mortgage.
