I am currently weighing my options between purchasing a retirement property in Bulgaria versus Spain, focusing primarily on upfront budget and long-term upkeep. While Spain offers a fantastic Mediterranean climate and established infrastructure, Bulgaria seems significantly cheaper to enter, though I am unsure about hidden maintenance costs and winter heating bills. Could anyone who has experience living in or buying property in both countries share insights on how the daily cost of living and climate compare for expats?
