Illiquid Properties in Bulgaria: Why Some Resort Apartments Take Years to Sell

PhantomPixel
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 04:22
I've been tracking the Bulgarian Black Sea property market for a while, and it seems like certain resort apartments in places like Sunny Beach or Bansko remain listed for years without finding buyers. Are these units simply overpriced by optimistic owners, or are there deeper structural issues like high maintenance fees, poor building quality, or legal complexities that make them completely illiquid? I'm curious if anyone here has successfully managed to sell a stagnant resort property or if people are just stuck paying annual maintenance fees forever with no exit strategy.
