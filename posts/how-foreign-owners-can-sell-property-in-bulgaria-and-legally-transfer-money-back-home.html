How Foreign Owners Can Sell Property in Bulgaria and Legally Transfer Money Back Home

StormBreaker
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.01.2025 03:18
I bought a holiday apartment in Bansko a few years ago, but now I'm looking to sell it and move the funds back to my home bank account outside the EU. What is the exact legal procedure for completing the sale as a non-resident, and which documents are required to prove origin of funds? Are there any specific tax clearance certificates I need to obtain from the Bulgarian authorities before my bank will allow the international wire transfer?

You will definitely need a Bulgarian tax clearance certificate (Uverenie) proving you owe no local real estate taxes or garbage collection fees to the municipality before the notary will sign the final title deed.
CosmicDrifter
Replied: 17.04.2025 08:48
When transferring money outside the EU, your Bulgarian bank will ask for the Notary Deed of sale, the preliminary contract, and proof of origin of funds, which is essentially the original bank statement showing how you originally brought the money into Bulgaria when you bought it.

Make sure your BULSTAT card is active. As a non-resident foreign owner, you were assigned a BULSTAT number when you bought the property, and the notary will need it during the sale process.
