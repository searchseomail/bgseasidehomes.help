Foreign Buyer Restrictions in Bulgaria: What Non-EU Citizens Can and Cannot Own

SolarFlare99
Topic Starter
Posted: 07.03.2025 04:07
I am a non-EU citizen looking to invest in real estate in Bulgaria, specifically a holiday apartment near the coast and a small plot of agricultural land. I've heard conflicting information about whether foreigners are allowed to own land directly or if I need to set up a local company first. Could someone clarify the exact legal restrictions on property ownership for third-country nationals in Bulgaria?

Replies (9)

PhantomPixel
Replied: 15.04.2025 12:40
As a non-EU citizen, you can definitely buy the apartment directly in your own name without setting up any company. Buildings and apartments are fully accessible to foreigners.
NeonVortex
Replied: 10.05.2025 13:18
However, the agricultural land is a completely different story. Under Bulgarian law, third-country nationals are strictly prohibited from owning land directly, which includes agricultural plots and forests.
ZenithGamer
Replied: 16.06.2025 00:34
To get around the land restriction, most non-EU buyers set up a Bulgarian EOOD (single-person limited liability company). The company can legally purchase the agricultural land for you.
GigaByte
Replied: 15.08.2025 08:53
Before you jump into buying land through a company, make sure you understand the ongoing accounting and tax maintenance costs. If you are also looking at urban areas, you might want to read up on Long-Term Rental Yields in Bulgarian Big Cities: Realistic Calculations and Taxes to see if city properties offer better returns than the coast.
TurboGlitch

Make sure you hire a reputable local independent lawyer who specializes in property law for foreigners. Do not rely solely on the agency selling you the property.
