Distressed Property Sales in Bulgaria: Where to Find Direct Below-Market Deals from Owners

RetroWave
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 07:41
I am currently looking to invest in the Bulgarian real estate market and want to focus specifically on distressed properties or direct sales from owners looking for a quick exit. Traditional agencies seem to inflate prices, so I am wondering where the best places are to find these below-market opportunities. Are there specific local websites, auctions, or networking strategies that experienced buyers use to connect directly with motivated sellers in areas like Sofia, Plovdiv, or the Black Sea coast?

Traditional agencies definitely add a heavy markup, especially in Sofia and along the coast. If you want direct deals, you need to look at local classifieds like OLX.bg and Bazar.bg where owners post directly without brokers.
