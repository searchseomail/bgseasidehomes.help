Topic Starter

I am currently looking into high-end real estate investments along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, specifically focusing on premium gated communities in areas like Sozopol, Nessebar, and near Varna. Are these luxury developments holding their value well, and what is the general consensus on property management and rental yields for high-net-worth buyers? I would love to hear insights from anyone with recent experience in this specific coastal market segment.
