Plovdiv Real Estate Market: Promising Investment Hub or Overpriced Housing?

BlueNova
Topic Starter
Posted: 27.02.2025 02:57
I've been looking into buying property in Plovdiv recently, but I'm torn on whether the current prices are justified. With all the new IT hubs and industrial zones expanding, demand seems high, yet local wages don't quite match the soaring cost per square meter. Are we looking at a booming investment opportunity, or is this a speculative bubble waiting to burst?
