Does Buying Real Estate in Bulgaria Grant You a Residence Permit (TRP)?

StellarPulse
Topic Starter
Posted: 23.02.2025 06:32
I am currently looking into property investment options in Eastern Europe and have been considering Sunny Beach in Bulgaria. Could someone please clarify if simply purchasing a residential apartment or house is enough to secure a Temporary Residence Permit (TRP)? I have heard conflicting information about minimum investment thresholds and whether renting out the property affects the application process.

Replies (10)

StaticPulse
Replied: 19.03.2025 01:38
No, simply buying a property in Bulgaria does not automatically grant you a Temporary Residence Permit (TRP). There is actually a minimum investment threshold of 600,000 BGN (about 300,000 EUR) in real estate to qualify for residency through investment.
