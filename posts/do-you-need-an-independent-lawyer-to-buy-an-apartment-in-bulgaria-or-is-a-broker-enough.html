Do You Need an Independent Lawyer to Buy an Apartment in Bulgaria or Is a Broker Enough?

ByteRunner
Topic Starter
Posted: 15.01.2025 00:28
I am currently looking at purchasing a resale apartment in Sofia and the real estate agency keeps telling me that their in-house legal team handles all the necessary checks. However, I am naturally skeptical about relying solely on the broker's legal department since they represent the transaction as a whole and potentially the seller's interests too. Is it standard practice in Bulgaria to hire an independent lawyer for title checks and contract drafting, or is a broker really enough to complete the purchase safely?
