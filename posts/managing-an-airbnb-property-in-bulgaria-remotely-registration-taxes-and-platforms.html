Topic Starter

Hey everyone! I recently purchased an apartment in Bansko and I'm planning to list it on Airbnb, but I live abroad and need to manage everything remotely. Could anyone walk me through the mandatory local registration process with the municipality and the Bulgarian Tourism Register for foreign owners? Also, I'm confused about how income taxes and VAT work for short-term rentals here, and which property management software integrates best with both Airbnb and local booking channels.

First things first, you'll need a BULSTAT number (UIC) from the Bulgarian Registry Agency if you're registering as a sole trader (ET) or company, which is usually recommended for non-residents.
ShadowStalker
Replied: 07.03.2025 02:54
For the municipality registration, you have to visit the local община (municipality) in Bansko within 7 days of starting operations to declare the tourist tax.
IronPhoenix
Replied: 19.04.2025 14:27
Regarding the Bulgarian Tourism Register (NRT), your property needs an official certificate. You can apply online through the Ministry of Tourism portal if you have a qualified electronic signature (KEP).
RetroWave
Replied: 22.05.2025 00:07
Since you live abroad, getting a KEP (electronic signature) might be tricky, so your local accountant or a legal representative with a power of attorney can handle the submission for you.
StellarPulse
Replied: 14.06.2025 09:57
Taxes-wise, the flat income tax in Bulgaria is 10%. If you register as a physical person renting out, you pay patent tax (патентен данък) which is a fixed annual amount depending on the location and bed capacity.
RetroWave
Replied: 01.07.2025 02:10
VAT is only mandatory if your turnover exceeds 166,000 BGN (roughly 85,000 EUR) in a 12-month period, so for a single apartment, you likely won't need to worry about VAT registration right away.
NeonVortex

Make sure your apartment has Act 16 (permission to use). Without it, getting the municipal classification certificate for short-term rental is practically impossible.
