Renting Out Your Apartment via Property Management Companies in Bulgaria: Fees and Reviews

QuantumSpark
Topic Starter
Posted: 11.02.2025 05:07
I recently bought a two-bedroom apartment in Sofia and am considering using a local property management company to handle long-term rentals while I am abroad. What are the typical commission fees and hidden costs I should expect in the Bulgarian market? I would also love to hear honest reviews or personal experiences regarding agencies like Unique Estates, Address, or smaller boutique firms.

Replies (12)

HyperSonic
Replied: 02.04.2025 08:39
Congratulations on your purchase in Sofia! For long-term rentals, most agencies charge between 8% to 10% of the monthly rent as a management fee. Just make sure to read the contract carefully so you don't get surprised by vacancy clauses.
SolarFlare99
Replied: 04.04.2025 12:12
I used Address for my apartment in Lozenets. Their commission was one month's rent for finding the tenant, plus 10% monthly for ongoing management. Communication was okay, but you definitely need to stay on top of them regarding maintenance issues.

Unique Estates is definitely on the higher end of the market. They usually deal with luxury properties and their fees reflect that—sometimes up to a full month's rent plus a higher percentage for management. But they do attract high-caliber tenants.
SolarFlare99

Watch out for hidden costs like 'tenant replacement fees' if the previous renter leaves early. Some agencies try to charge you another half-month commission even if it hasn't been a full year yet.
